# WP EasyCart store design, for your AI

Teaches ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini how a WP EasyCart store's Modern storefront is built and how to answer in the change format
the Store Designer reads ( WPEC-CHANGES v1 ). It holds nothing about your store: your AI also needs your store's design guide,
from WP EasyCart › Store Designer › Use your AI › Download the guide.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `INSTRUCTIONS.md` | The instructions to give a ChatGPT project or a Gemini Gem |
| `SKILL.md` | The same, as a Claude skill |
| `references/change-format.md` | The change format and the store's checks, for orientation ( your guide wins where they differ ) |

## ChatGPT

1. Make a project, and paste `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into its instructions.
2. Add your store's design guide to the project's files ( and `references/change-format.md` if you like ).
3. In a chat in the project, ask for what you want. The Store Designer's Use your AI tab has a starter prompt.

## Gemini

1. Make a Gem, and paste `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into its instructions.
2. Add your store's design guide to its knowledge.
3. Ask the Gem for what you want.

## Claude

Upload this zip in Claude's settings ( Skills ), then attach your store's guide to a chat. Or download the Claude skill from the
Use your AI tab: it already holds your store's guide. With your store connected to Claude ( the WP EasyCart for Claude plugin,
or a custom connector to your store's MCP server ), Claude reads the guide itself and makes the draft for you.

## Then

Paste your AI's answer into the Store Designer ( Use your AI › Your AI's changes ) and press Check and make a draft. Look the
draft over in the preview and publish when you are ready. Only paste changes you asked your own AI for. Never paste code someone
sent you.

Docs: https://docs.wpeasycart.com/
